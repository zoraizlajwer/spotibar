Spotibar

Spotify controls, right on your taskbar.

Spotibar is a Rainmeter skin that puts Spotify playback controls, album-art accent colors and an audio visualizer on your Windows taskbar. Six skins, Light and Dark.

Features

Small footprint. Big control.

Everything you reach for while music plays, one hover away from wherever you are in Windows.

Smart launch and minimize

Open Spotify from the skin's own icon. When Spotify closes, the skin folds neatly back into a spotibar panel.

Dynamic panels

Hover over the cover to reveal the controls panel and the song info panel, depending on the layout you chose.

Quick access

Click the cover or the skin to bring the main Spotify window straight to the foreground.

Full playback controls

Previous, Play/Pause, Next and Repeat. The repeat button also shows whether you are repeating the queue or a single track.

Volume gestures

Scroll the wheel or two-finger swipe to change volume. Right-click to mute and unmute without opening anything.

Adaptive accent colors

The progress ring, song title and visulizer pull an accent color from the current album art and change with every track.

Audio visualizer

Ten smooth bars react to your system audio output, tinted with the accent color of whatever is playing.

Light and Dark themes

Match your Windows taskbar. Every layout ships in both, and hover icons glow in Spotify green.

Works with Spotify Web

Use the browser player through the WebNowPlaying extension. Desktop app is preferred.

Get started

Installed in under two minutes.

Spotibar bundles the plugins it needs, so there is nothing else to download.

1

Install the basics

Get Spotify for Windows and Rainmeter if you do not have them yet.

2

Download the package

Grab Spotibar_v3.0.rmskin from the GitHub Releases page.

3

Double-click to install

Rainmeter opens the installer. Choose Install and the skin menu loads automatically.

4

Pick a skin and play

Click a skin in the menu, start Spotify, play a song and hover over the cover.

Using Spotify in the browser? Install the WebNowPlaying extension, Desktop app is preferred.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does Spotibar work with the Spotify web player?
Yes. Install the WebNowPlaying browser extension. While using the skin with the Spotify web player, the song cover, progress bar, and controls do not work. I haven't found any fix for this issue.
Do I need to install any plugins?
No. The WebNowPlaying and Chameleon plugins are bundled in the .rmskin package and install with the skin.
The skin covers my fullscreen game. What can I do?
Spotibar uses Stay topmost to sit above the taskbar. Open Rainmeter, go to Manage > Game Mode, enable Fullscreen games, set On start to Unload all skins and On stop to Reload saved Spotibar layout.
The visualizer is not moving.
The visualizer listens to your default Windows audio output. Make sure Spotify is playing through that device, then refresh the skin from the Rainmeter manager.
The buttons do not respond to clicks.
Open the skin's settings and make sure Click through is unchecked. Also keep Draggable unchecked so clicks are not treated as drags.
Where does the accent color come from?
The Chameleon plugin reads the dominant colors from the current album cover. The progress ring, cover border and song title use it, and it updates on every track change.
Can I use or modify Spotibar?
Yes. Spotibar is released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 license. Share and adapt it freely, just credit the original author. Colors, corner radius and the volume step live in @Resources/Variables.inc.
Is it affiliated with Spotify?
No. Spotibar is an independent, community project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Spotify. Spotify is a trademark of Spotify AB.
